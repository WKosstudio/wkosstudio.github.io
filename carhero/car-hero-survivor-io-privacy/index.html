Privacy Policy

Car Hero Survivor IO · WKos
Effective and last updated: November 8, 2026

About this policy

This policy describes how Car Hero Survivor IO (Android package com.wkos.car.hero.survivor.io), its website and support handle information. The services described below process data when their features are used. Processing depends on the enabled services, your region, device settings and applicable privacy choices.

1. Who is responsible and how to contact us

WKos is the developer responsible for Car Hero Survivor IO and for the information we handle to provide support. For privacy questions or requests, email wkos.studio@gmail.com with the subject “Car Hero — Privacy”.

2. Game progress and preferences

The game stores progress, garage upgrades, game resources, preferences and purchase entitlements locally on your device. Purchase records include a transaction fingerprint to prevent duplicate delivery of items. These records help the game resume progress and remember purchases.

Local data remains until it is reset or cleared. Android backup settings may preserve or restore app data independently of WKos. Clearing local data may lose progress; completed consumable purchases may not be recoverable. The game does not require a separate WKos account.

3. Website and support information

This game's web pages do not include advertising pixels, third-party analytics, sign-up forms or tracking cookies. They are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, under its Privacy Statement. Following an external Google Play or provider link lets that provider process information under its own policy.

If you contact support, WKos receives your email address, any name you provide, your message and any attachments or diagnostic information you choose to send. We use this information to answer you, investigate problems, handle privacy requests and protect against abuse. Our support mailbox is hosted by Google's Gmail service; see Google's Privacy Policy. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or unrelated sensitive information.

5. Sharing, legal bases and privacy choices

We share information only with the providers needed for the enabled functions described above, to respond to you, or when required by law or to protect legal rights. WKos does not sell support messages or use them for advertising. Advertising providers may use their data for advertising and measurement under their policies; where applicable, you can opt out of personalized ads and of processing treated as sale or sharing under local law.

Where applicable, we rely on providing the service you request for gameplay, support and purchases, legitimate interests for necessary security and service improvement, legal obligations for required records, and consent for processing that requires it, including certain analytics and personalized advertising. Required consent must be obtained before that processing starts. You may withdraw consent through the privacy controls in the game (section 6) or contact us; withdrawal does not invalidate earlier lawful processing. Android and Google account settings also provide advertising and profile controls.

6. Privacy controls in the game

At the first start, before the game shows ads or asks for advertising consent, the game opens a Privacy and terms window with links to this Privacy Policy and to the Terms of Use. Tapping Accept records on your device which version of the documents you accepted and when. If a document changes materially, the window asks again.

In the game, open Settings → Privacy at any time to read this policy and the Terms of Use, and, where the law of your region requires it, to open Advertising privacy and review or change your advertising choices. The advertising consent and privacy choices are provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform, a consent management platform certified for the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework.

Google Analytics for Firebase follows the same choice: until a decision is available, analytics and advertising storage are set to “denied” on your device; where no consent is required, they are set to “granted”, and where consent is required, they follow your answer in the consent form.

7. European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through the consent form before advertising partners and analytics store or access information on your device or use it for personalized advertising and measurement. You can refuse, choose individual purposes and partners, or withdraw consent later in Settings → Privacy → Advertising privacy. If you refuse, the game may still show non-personalized or limited ads where the providers allow them.

WKos is the controller for the information it receives for support and privacy requests, as described in section 1. The legal bases are listed in section 5. You have the rights listed in section 9, including the right to lodge a complaint with the data-protection supervisory authority of your country of residence or work.

8. United States: state privacy rights

This section applies to residents of U.S. states with comprehensive privacy laws, such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas and Oregon, to the extent those laws apply to WKos.

Categories of personal information. Through the services in section 4, the game may involve: identifiers (advertising ID, app-instance and game-scoped player identifiers, IP address); internet or network activity (ad views and interactions, gameplay and diagnostic events); approximate location derived from the IP address; commercial information (purchase records and entitlements); and, for support, the contact information and messages you send. The sources are your device, your use of the game and you. The purposes are described in sections 2–5. Retention is described in section 9.

Targeted advertising, sale and sharing. WKos does not sell personal information for money. Advertising partners may use device identifiers and ad interactions for targeted advertising, which some state laws treat as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information or as targeted advertising. You can opt out in Settings → Privacy → Advertising privacy when the game shows that option for your state, by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android settings, or by emailing us. The game does not use or disclose sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you, and it does not knowingly sell or share personal information of consumers under 16.

Your rights. Depending on your state, you may request to know or access the personal information we hold, correct it, delete it, receive a portable copy, and opt out of targeted advertising, sale or sharing, and of profiling with legal or similarly significant effects (the game does not perform such profiling). Email wkos.studio@gmail.com; we answer within 45 days, or explain why we need an extension where the law allows it. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with your signed permission; we may ask you to confirm the request directly. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Appeals. If we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the subject “Car Hero — Privacy appeal”. We will answer the appeal within the period required by your state law (for example, 45 or 60 days). If you disagree with the result, you may contact the attorney general of your state.

9. Retention, deletion and your rights

Request deletion

  1. Email wkos.studio@gmail.com with the subject “Car Hero — Privacy deletion”.
  2. Identify Car Hero Survivor IO and describe which information you want deleted. Include only the information needed to locate the records; never send your password or full card details.
  3. We review your request, delete WKos-held support messages and attachments where applicable, and route requests for data processed on our behalf to the relevant provider when records can be identified. We explain any legally required retention or information we need to complete the request. Provider-controlled Google account, Play Games and transaction records are managed through those providers’ privacy controls.

Local game data stays on your device until it is reset or cleared. Use Android Settings → Apps → Car Hero Survivor IO → Storage → Clear storage to remove local progress and preferences; this loses local progress. Manage any Android backups separately. Clearing local data does not automatically erase records held by Google or other enabled providers.

Support messages are kept while needed to address your request, follow up on the issue or establish, exercise or defend a legal claim, then deleted when no longer needed. We retain records required by law for the legally required period. You can request deletion sooner by emailing wkos.studio@gmail.com. We will explain any information that must be retained and the reason.

Analytics retention is governed by the configured Firebase/Google Analytics retention setting; advertising, store transaction and profile records follow each provider's policy and applicable law. Unity IAP 5.4+ service retention and developer-mediated access/deletion procedures depend on the features enabled. See Unity IAP 5.4+ data and retention details. Google Play Games data can be managed or deleted through your Play Games profile; Google purchase history and legally required transaction records are controlled by Google.

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction of personal information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent and complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us without sending unnecessary identity documents. We may request the minimum information needed to verify the request and locate the relevant records. The game does not require a separate WKos account; contact us about data we hold, and use the provider controls for your Google account.

10. Security and international processing

We limit access to support information and use the protections of our hosting and email providers. The listed SDK providers document encrypted network transport. We use HTTPS for network communications. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure. Google, GitHub, Unity and Sentry may process information outside your country under their published safeguards; their linked policies explain the relevant arrangements.

11. Age and children

The Google Play target audience is adults aged 18 and older. This is an audience choice, not an announced store content rating. The game is not intended to solicit children's personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so that we can investigate and delete it where required.

12. Changes

We will update this policy and its date when practices change. Material changes and any new consent requirements will be communicated before the changed processing begins. Read the game's privacy controls and Google Play Data safety information alongside this policy.
