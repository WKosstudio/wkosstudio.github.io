Privacy Policy

Car Hero Survivor IO · WKos
Effective and last updated: October 12, 2026

Pre-release notice

Car Hero Survivor IO (Android package com.wkos.car.hero.survivor.io) is in development and has not been publicly released. This policy covers this game's website, support communications and the current pre-release game, and separately describes planned release services. A planned service is not a claim that it is already running in the current build.

Google AdMob advertising, Google Analytics for Firebase, Google Play Games sign-in with guest play and in-app purchases are planned. Unity LevelPlay/ironSource mediation may be introduced later. The enabled services and their consent choices will be disclosed in the game and this policy before they begin processing data.

1. Who is responsible and how to contact us

WKos is the developer responsible for Car Hero Survivor IO and for the information we handle to provide support. For privacy questions or requests, email wkos.studio@gmail.com with the subject “Car Hero — Privacy”.

2. Data in the current pre-release game

The current game stores progress, garage upgrades, game resources and preferences locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs. This enables the game to resume your progress and remember settings. The inspected game implementation writes gameplay events to local diagnostic logs; it does not implement a remote analytics service. Advertising delivery, Google Play Games sign-in and the purchase payment flow are not yet connected in that implementation.

Local progress remains until you reset or clear the game's data. Android backup settings may preserve or restore app data independently of WKos. We do not operate a player-account or cloud-save service in the current build. Installing a development package alone does not mean its optional online services are active.

3. Website and support information

This game's web pages do not include advertising pixels, third-party analytics, sign-up forms or tracking cookies. They are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, under its Privacy Statement. Following an external Google Play or provider link lets that provider process information under its own policy.

If you contact support, WKos receives your email address, any name you provide, your message and any attachments or diagnostic information you choose to send. We use this information to answer you, investigate problems, handle privacy requests and protect against abuse. Our support mailbox is hosted by Google's Gmail service; see Google's Privacy Policy. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or unrelated sensitive information.

5. Sharing, legal bases and privacy choices

We share information only with the providers needed for the enabled functions described above, to respond to you, or when required by law or to protect legal rights. WKos does not sell support messages or use them for advertising. Advertising providers may use their data for advertising and measurement under their policies; where applicable, you can opt out of personalized ads and of processing treated as sale or sharing under local law.

Where applicable, we rely on providing the service you request for gameplay, support and purchases, legitimate interests for necessary security and service improvement, legal obligations for required records, and consent for processing that requires it, including certain analytics and personalized advertising. Required consent must be obtained before that processing starts. You may withdraw consent through the privacy controls offered in the released game or contact us; withdrawal does not invalidate earlier lawful processing. Android and Google account settings also provide advertising and profile controls.

6. Retention, deletion and your rights

Local game data stays on your device until it is reset or cleared. Use Android Settings → Apps → Car Hero Survivor IO → Storage → Clear storage to remove local progress and preferences; this loses local progress. Manage any Android backups separately. Clearing local data does not automatically erase records held by Google or other enabled providers.

Support messages are kept while needed to address your request, follow up on the issue or establish, exercise or defend a legal claim, then deleted when no longer needed. We retain records required by law for the legally required period. You can request deletion sooner by emailing wkos.studio@gmail.com. We will explain any information that must be retained and the reason.

Once enabled, analytics retention is governed by the configured Firebase/Google Analytics retention setting; advertising, store transaction and profile records follow each provider's policy and applicable law. These launch settings are still being configured. We will publish the relevant configuration before collection begins. If Unity IAP cloud-commerce services are enabled, their 5.4+ implementation can additionally process player/installation/session identifiers, device information and country for commerce operations; this is not a currently enabled service. See Unity IAP 5.4+ data and retention details. Google Play Games data can be managed or deleted through your Play Games profile; Google purchase history and legally required transaction records are controlled by Google.

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction of personal information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent and complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us without sending unnecessary identity documents. We may request the minimum information needed to verify the request and locate the relevant records. A separate WKos account and account-creation flow are not currently available; contact us about data we hold, and use the provider controls for your Google account.

7. Security and international processing

We limit access to support information and use the protections of our hosting and email providers. Planned network services must use encrypted transport where supported and required. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure. Google, GitHub and Unity may process information outside your country under their published safeguards; their linked policies explain the relevant arrangements.

8. Age and children

The planned Google Play target audience is adults aged 18 and older. This is an audience choice, not an announced store content rating. The game is not intended to solicit children's personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so that we can investigate and delete it where required. We will review age and advertising settings before release.

9. Changes

We will update this policy and its date when practices change. Material changes and any new consent requirements will be communicated before the changed processing begins. Read the released game's privacy controls and Google Play Data safety information alongside this policy.
