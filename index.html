About WKos

Indie dev, chaos creator, and "I'll do it myself" enthusiast.

Hi, I'm Vadym Kostariev, and WKos is the name I work under, even if it is just me for now. I once imagined this whole thing growing into a studio big enough to make someone at Tencent pause for a second and ask where this guy came from.

Nobody there is panicking yet. I am still the one coding, drawing, designing, testing, inventing mechanics, and rescheduling deadlines like it is part of the craft. But I keep going because I genuinely enjoy making games, even when common sense quietly disagrees.
