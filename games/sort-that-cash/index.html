About This Game

First day on the job. One vault. Over a million banknotes.

The bank needs someone to put its money in order. That someone is you. Behind a massive round door lie mountains of dollars, euros, pounds and yuan. There's a floor under there somewhere. By the end of your shift, you'll have found it.

Sort That Cash! is a first-person game about turning cash-covered chaos into satisfying order. The piles shrink, empty shelves fill up, and you can see the difference you've made.

One hundred notes. One strap. One place to put them.

Grab a handful. Pick out notes of the same currency and denomination. Got a hundred? Strap them into a bundle and carry it to its shelf. That's the whole job. Now do it another ten thousand times or so.

Every bundle in the right place earns you money. Fill a shelf, rack or pallet for a bonus.

Put the machines to work

Load mixed cash into a sorting machine and set it up for the job. It sorts the notes and straps them into bundles of a hundred. Bring in the next load while it works. Spend your earnings on better equipment, and even the biggest piles start melting away.

Suck it up. Blow it out.

Vacuum loose notes off the floor, then blow them where you want them. Or just blow them everywhere.

There's also a money gun if you feel like making it rain. You're still the one cleaning up.

What's in the boxes?

Find keys and peek inside private safe deposit boxes. Some clients keep things more interesting than money.

At your own pace

Take your time or find the fastest way through the next pile. Show or hide the time display as you like. Just you, the rustle of banknotes, and another shelf filled.

Just don’t try to walk out with any of it. Security takes that very seriously.

Features

  • Over a million banknotes in four currencies and 16 denominations.
  • Three tiers of sorting machines and vacuum-blowers.
  • Gold and platinum bars.
  • Controller support.
  • Steam Achievements. For anyone who wants the ultimate sorting challenge: Beat the Sorting Genre. Strap every bundle by hand, without a sorting machine.
