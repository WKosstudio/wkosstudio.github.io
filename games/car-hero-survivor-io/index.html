CAR SURVIVAL ON ANDROID

A roguelite car shooter with automatic weapons

Car Hero Survivor IO is a 3D car survival game that combines arcade driving with automatic shooting. In this top-down car shooter, you steer through waves of enemy vehicles while your weapons keep firing. Find a gap, collect experience and choose the next upgrade for your build.

A laser, roof turret, drones and an energy field give you different ways to clear space. Random upgrade offers shape each run, while permanent garage upgrades strengthen your car and weapons between attempts. Survive the pressure, gather cores and prepare for the sector boss.

If you enjoy wave survival games and experimenting with weapon builds, this auto shooter puts the arsenal on wheels. Your movement, positioning and upgrade choices decide how long you stay in the fight.

About the car survival gameplay

How do you shoot in Car Hero Survivor IO?

Weapons fire automatically. You control the car, find space between enemy vehicles and choose upgrades as you level up.

How does roguelite progression work?

Choose random weapon and improvement offers during a run. Spend earned cores on permanent car and weapon upgrades in the garage to prepare for future attempts.
