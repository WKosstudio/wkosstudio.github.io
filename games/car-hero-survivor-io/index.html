Drive through the fight

Find gaps in the enemy crowd. Combine a laser, roof turret, drones and an energy field to keep your route open.

Move from node to node, gather cores and prepare your build for the final fight with the sector guardian.

Come back stronger

Adapt to random upgrade offers during a run. Spend the cores you bring home on lasting car and weapon improvements in the garage.
