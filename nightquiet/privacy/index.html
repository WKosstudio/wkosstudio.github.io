Night Quiet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy applies to Night Quiet for Android TV (package ua.nightquiet.tv), provided by Vadym Kostariev (WKos studio).

What the app does

Night Quiet lets you set a sleep timer, choose a bedtime, and optionally start a timer after your TV boots or wakes. It runs locally on your Android TV device.

Data collection and sharing

Night Quiet does not collect or share personal data. The app has no internet permission and does not send your timer settings, viewing activity, device information, or usage data to us or to third parties. It contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. No account is required or created.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your selected theme, timer duration or bedtime, current countdown state, auto-timer preference, and recent timer result on your device. When the system screen timeout is temporarily changed to request sleep, its previous value is saved locally so it can be restored. This information is used only to operate the app and is not transmitted off your device.

Settings remain until you change them, clear the app's storage through Android settings, or uninstall the app. The app disables Android application backup. If you have enabled the optional device administrator permission, revoke it in Night Quiet or Android settings before uninstalling.

Permissions and system access

  • Alarms and reminders: schedules the sleep action and final-minute reminder at the time you choose.
  • Modify system settings: temporarily lowers the screen-off timeout on devices using the system-timeout sleep method, then restores the previous value.
  • Display over other apps: displays the final-minute reminder and allows the dark sleep screen to open over another app where required by Android.
  • Optional device administrator: requests only screen locking to put supported devices into standby. Night Quiet does not request device wiping, password policies, or access to your files.
  • Notifications: provides timer status and controls. The optional auto timer uses an ongoing, silent notification while its foreground service is enabled.
  • Run after startup: restores the optional auto timer if you previously enabled it. Manual timers are cleared after reboot.

The optional auto-timer service observes screen on/off events on the device to start your chosen countdown after wake. It does not monitor which videos you watch. You can disable Auto timer in the app's settings or through its notification. You can review or revoke special access in Android settings.

Children

Night Quiet does not collect personal information from users of any age.

Contacting the developer

For privacy questions, contact wkos.studio@gmail.com. If you choose to email us, your email provider sends the information you include in that message. We use that information to respond to your request; this is separate from the app, which does not transmit data.

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated with a new revision date.
