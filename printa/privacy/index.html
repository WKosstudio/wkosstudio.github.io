PrintA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy applies to PrintA for Android (package ua.printa.app), provided by Vadym Kostariev (WKos studio).

What the app does

PrintA prints receipts, PDF files, images and text on thermal receipt printers that you connect over a local network, Bluetooth or USB. It can also appear as a printer in the standard Android print dialog.

Data collection and sharing

PrintA does not collect or share personal data. The documents you print are converted on your device and sent only to the printer you selected. They are not sent to us or to third parties. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. No account is required or created.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your printer settings on your device: the connection type, the printer's network address, Bluetooth address or USB identifiers, paper width, print quality options and similar preferences. A document being printed may be kept in a temporary file while it is prepared and is deleted afterwards. Settings remain until you change them, clear the app's storage through Android settings, or uninstall the app. The app disables Android application backup.

Purchases

The free version prints a note with the app's logo at the bottom of each receipt saying that it was printed with the free version of PrintA. An optional one-time purchase removes this note. The note is also not printed when the system language of your device is Ukrainian; to decide this, PrintA reads the device's language setting, which is used on the device only and is not sent anywhere. Purchases are processed by Google Play; PrintA receives only the confirmation that the purchase belongs to your Google account and does not receive your payment details. Google's handling of purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions and system access

  • Network access: connects to the printer on your local network and searches the local network for printers when you ask for it. Purchases use Google Play.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): lists printers paired with your phone, searches for Bluetooth LE printers when you ask for it, and connects to the printer you select. PrintA does not use Bluetooth to determine your location.
  • Location (Android 11 and older only): Android requires this permission for a Bluetooth LE search on those versions. PrintA does not read or store your location.
  • USB devices: sends print data to a printer you connect by cable, after you allow access in the Android dialog.
  • Files you choose: PrintA reads only the files you open in it or share to it.

Children

PrintA does not collect personal information from users of any age.

Contacting the developer

For privacy questions, contact wkos.studio@gmail.com. If you choose to email us, your email provider sends the information you include in that message. We use that information to respond to your request; this is separate from the app, which does not transmit data.

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated with a new revision date.
